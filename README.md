# New Grad 2027: early career jobs and programs

**1,647 open new grad and entry level roles at 593 US companies.** Checked 15 Oct 2026. 420 posted in the last 7 days. 576 at employers with a visa sponsorship record.

Every role comes straight from the employer's own job board, picked up by [Litos](https://trylitos.com/?utm_source=github&utm_campaign=ng-readme&utm_medium=readme). Nothing is copied from LinkedIn or Indeed. Rebuilt every Monday, so closed roles drop off and new ones come in.

Only roles a new grad can actually apply to: titles that say new grad, graduate, entry level, early career, junior, "I" levels (Engineer I, Analyst I), or a named early career program. No senior titles, no internships.

## How to use these lists

- Click a role to open it on Litos. The page shows the job and the company's own apply link.
- Need sponsorship? The visa signal column shows employers whose posting says they sponsor, or who have US H-1B (USCIS) or LCA (DOL) records. A record means the employer has sponsored before, not that a given role will, so always confirm with the employer.

Star this repo to keep it handy, and watch it to get the Monday update. Looking for internships? See [Hiring-Now](https://github.com/mehek-builds/Hiring-Now).
